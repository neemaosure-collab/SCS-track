## 1. Where files live
All operational, financial, and team files live in company-owned Shared Drives rather than individual My Drive folders so work remains accessible if staff leave. Personal My Drive is restricted to temporary drafts only.

## 2. Sharing outside the business
External sharing is set to warn users when sharing outside the organization, and visitors cannot edit or reshare files unless explicitly approved by a manager. Public link sharing ("Anyone with the link") is turned off by default.

## 3. Who can sign in
Only active staff in the practice organizational unit may sign in, and 2-Step Verification (2SV) is enforced for all accounts. Unapproved mobile or desktop endpoints require basic device management approval before syncing company data.

## 4. What we keep, how long, where
Active business records, invoices, and customer logs are kept in Shared Drives for 7 years (to be checked against local tax rules), while temporary working drafts are reviewed quarterly. Deleted files remain in Drive trash for 30 days before permanent removal.

## 5. The monthly export
On the last working day of each month, an administrator runs a Google Takeout or Admin data export of critical Shared Drive folders. The exported archive is verified by test-restoring one file and stored offline on an encrypted company drive.

## 6. Who holds admin
Two trusted partners hold Super Admin access using dedicated admin accounts protected by hardware/app 2-Step Verification and offline paper backup codes. Daily staff work is performed on standard non-admin user accounts.

## 7. When someone leaves
On a leaver's final day, their account is suspended immediately, active sign-in sessions and app passwords are revoked, and their My Drive files and calendar ownership are transferred to their manager. The suspended account is kept for 30 days before deletion.
